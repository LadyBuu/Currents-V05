## 1. Overview

The relationship domain manages every non-family interpersonal connection between characters: romance, friendship, rivalry, enmity, professional relationships, affairs, jealousy, and matchmaking.

Family relationships — parentage, siblingship, kinship — do **not** live here. Parentage lives in `FamilyState`. Kinship is derived by `FamilyRules.classifyKinship`. The relationship domain never stores or duplicates parentage.
